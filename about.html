About me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Water Resources Development and Management at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India, working at the intersection of hydrology, hydroclimatology, and artificial intelligence. My research explores how climate, catchment characteristics, and human activities shape hydrological extremes and streamflow dynamics across river basins.

I develop hydrological forecasting systems that combine physical understanding with machine-learning and deep-learning techniques — including floodplain mapping over data-scarce regions, flood-process characterisation, large-sample hydrology, streamflow forecasting, baseflow contribution to floods, and short-to-sub-seasonal forecasting with uncertainty quantification, aimed at improving water-resource management under changing environmental conditions.

I'm always open to discussing research ideas, collaborations, and opportunities in hydrology, climate science, and data-driven environmental modelling.
